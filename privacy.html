Privacy Policy — Bethel's

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Bethel's is made by Bethel Ventures for Bethel's Landscaping and Handyman Services. This policy explains, in plain words, what the app does with information.

The short version: nothing leaves the phone. Bethel's has no servers, no accounts, no ads, and no analytics. The app does not collect, sell, share, or send personal information anywhere.

How the app works

  • Bethel's is a back-office app for running the business: ballpark estimates, an estimates tracker, projects, invoices, and a price book. All of it works on the phone only.
  • Customer names, phone numbers, and job addresses typed into estimates, projects, and invoices are entered by the business owner to run the business, and they stay in the app on that phone.
  • The "Call" button on the Home screen opens the phone's own dialer with the business number filled in. That call is placed by the phone's dialer app, not by this app, and nothing about it is recorded by this app.

What the app stores, and where

  • Estimates, projects, invoices, and prices are stored in a private database on the phone only.
  • This information is never uploaded, emailed, or transmitted anywhere. There is no server for it to go to.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

What the app does not do

  • It does not take payments or ask for bank, card, or account information.
  • It does not access contacts, location, photos, microphone, or messages.
  • It does not show ads and contains no advertising or tracking software.

Children: Bethel's is a business tool made for adults. It is not directed at children.

Changes: If a future version changes how the app handles information, this policy will be updated and the new version posted at the same address before the change takes effect.
